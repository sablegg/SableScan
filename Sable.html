🧠 How Analysis Modes Work
```
BOTH (Recommended)
Runs Formula first, then passes all the price data to Claude AI for a second opinion. The buy price shown is the Formula result. The AI verdict below it either confirms, challenges, or adds context the numbers alone can't capture — things like "this card spikes every Q4" or "recent reprint announcement is suppressing price."
Best for: most buying decisions. Two calls per scan (~8–12 seconds). Highest accuracy.
FORMULA ONLY
Pure maths. Takes the weighted average of recent eBay sold prices across your chosen window (7d / 30d / 90d), then subtracts your margin percentage. No AI involved after identification. Fast, consistent, and cheap — ideal when you're at a show scanning dozens of cards and need instant, no-frills pricing.
Best for: bulk scanning, high volume, low margin cards. One API call per scan (~4–5 seconds). Cheapest.
AI ONLY
Claude AI evaluates the card holistically — it still receives the PokeTrace price data, but it weighs demand trends, set context, player vs collector appeal, reprint risk, and market sentiment to produce a qualitative buy recommendation rather than a hard number. Better for high-value or unusual cards where context matters more than the 30-day average.
Best for: vintage cards, alt arts, high-value singles where gut and context matter. Two API calls per scan (~10–15 seconds). No hard price — verdict and reasoning only.
Why we made it configurable: Formula is predictable and auditable — you can see exactly why it said $15.30. AI catches things numbers miss but costs more per call and takes longer. Both together gives you a sanity check in both directions. For most users, Both is the right default.
```
